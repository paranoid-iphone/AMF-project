# Future opportunities backlog

## Status

This is a parking lot for ideas outside the currently approved MVP. Items here are **not approved requirements** and must be refined, prioritized, and moved into their own feature specifications before implementation.

Some early entries below—document conflict detection, evidence statuses, risk handling, and audit history—have since moved into the approved target MVP. The authoritative sequence is now `docs/initial-feature-backlog.md`; this file remains a historical parking lot and must not override newer product or methodology documents.

## Evaluation expansion

- Add the operating-business assessment from `project-evaluation-criteria.docx`.
- Add the business-sale / express due-diligence assessment.
- Allow comparison between assessment attempts and show progress over time.
- Analyze presentations, business plans, and financial documents.
- Expand ingestion beyond text-based PDF/DOCX to spreadsheets, presentations, images, and scanned documents using format-specific extraction and OCR.
- Cite source passages from uploaded documents in AI conclusions.
- Detect contradictions between questionnaire answers and uploaded files.
- Introduce evidence status per answer: self-reported, document-supported, or manually verified.
- Define carefully governed stop factors where a total score alone is insufficient.

## Investor experience

- Investor registration and profile.
- Individual minimum passing score.
- Project discovery and filtering.
- Private summaries with controlled access to confidential details.
- Request-access workflow for full project materials.
- Save projects and create a review shortlist.
- Express interest or send a non-binding offer.
- Investor-specific preferences beyond a passing score, only if real usage demonstrates the need.

## Marketplace and collaboration

- Applicant-controlled project publication.
- Secure applicant–investor messaging.
- Notifications about investor interest and application status.
- Multiple users or advisers collaborating on one project.
- Agency or analyst role for assisted preparation and review.
- Telegram Mini App after the primary web workflow and Telegram notification channel are stable.

## Verification and due diligence

- Manual moderator or analyst review.
- Automated checks against permitted Kazakhstan data sources.
- Verification of legal entity, litigation, debt, licenses, and ownership where lawful and technically available.
- Document request checklists tailored to the project and deal type.
- Audit trail for assessments, evidence, and reviewer actions.

## Localization and commercialization

- Kazakh localization.
- English localization.
- Paid assessment, subscription, investor subscription, or success-fee experiments.
- Usage limits and paid tiers only after usage patterns are understood.

## High-complexity ideas requiring separate approval

- Legally binding offers inside the platform.
- Electronic signing of investment documents.
- Holding or transferring money.
- Escrow, payment processing, lending, securities, or investment intermediation.
- Automated investment decisions or claims that AI provides regulated financial advice.

These items may introduce material regulatory, security, privacy, and operational obligations in Kazakhstan and must not be added incrementally without dedicated product, legal, and architecture review.
