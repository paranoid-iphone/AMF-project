# Domain language

## Applicant

A person who owns or initiates an investment project and uses the platform to prepare and assess it. Applicant is preferred over borrower because the product supports equity investment as well as other financing structures.

## Project

An applicant-owned record describing one proposed new business project. In the initial product scope, every project is an SPV project.

## Owner

The applicant who created a project and has private access to it. Ownership does not imply ownership of every future asset or legal entity associated with the proposed SPV.

## Draft

A private project that the owner is still preparing. A draft can be incomplete and can be edited without a verified email.

## Active

A private project that the owner has marked as complete enough for the current lifecycle. Active does not mean visible to investors or the public.

## Activation

The owner's explicit transition of a complete project from Draft to Active. Activation requires a verified account email.

## Deactivation

The owner's explicit transition of a project from Active back to Draft.

## Published

A future project state in which an approved audience other than the owner can discover or view the project. Published is not part of the private project-draft lifecycle.

## Publication

The future controlled transition that makes a project visible to an approved audience. Publication is distinct from Activation.

## Notification channel

A verified and consented route used to notify a user, such as email, phone, or Telegram. A notification channel is associated with the user and is not a public project contact by default.
